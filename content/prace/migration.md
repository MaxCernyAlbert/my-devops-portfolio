### The Challenge

Fragmented pipelines across Jenkins and Azure DevOps caused high licensing costs and friction. The client needed a unified, modern platform.

### My Solution

Executed a complete, zero-downtime migration to GitHub Enterprise. Converted complex Groovy/YAML pipelines to optimized **GitHub Actions**. Standardized all provisioning via Terraform modules, reducing pipeline execution time by an average of 30%.
