My 4-Week Onboarding Protocol

Structured engagement designed to deliver value from day one.

W1
Discovery & Alignment: Environment access, architecture mapping, understanding business expectations and pain points.
W2
Quick Wins: Identifying and implementing immediate improvements. Often pays for itself.
W3
Strategic Roadmap: Presenting a 6-12 month plan for IaC adoption, automation, and migration priorities.
W4
First Production Delivery: Tangible output shipped—pipeline, module, or automation live in production.

Risk-Free Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with the audit and roadmap presented in Week 3, the contract can be terminated immediately with zero cost to you.
